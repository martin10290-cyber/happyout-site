First Time?

Everything you need to know before your first run.

Is it free?
Yes, every run is free. No membership, no fees.
Do I need to sign up?
Nope, just show up. Tapping "I'm in" on a run is optional, but it helps us know who's coming.
How fast do you go?
Every pace is welcome. Run your own pace, and we regroup at the end.
How far are the runs?
Usually 3 to 5 miles. Check each run on the schedule for details.
I'm new to running. Is this for me?
Absolutely. Walk breaks are totally fine, and nobody gets judged for their pace. Plenty of us started as beginners.
Where do we meet?
The meetup spot is listed on each run in the schedule. Tap "Add to Calendar" to save the time and place. For route details, join our Strava club, where we post each route.
Can I bring my dog or a stroller?
Yes, both are welcome.
What happens after the run?
We usually grab coffee and hang out. Stay as long as you like.
How do I stay in the loop?
Bookmark this page, and follow us on Instagram and Strava.

See you on the road, Long Beach. ☀️
